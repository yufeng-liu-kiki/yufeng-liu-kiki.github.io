Research

Language is never
just language.

My work sits at the intersection of language, health and society. I use corpus linguistics, discourse analysis and cognitive linguistics to study communication in healthcare, online communities, news media and AI-mediated settings.

My current research in the Horizon Europe 4D PICTURE project focuses on metaphor, identity and emotion in communication about cancer and chronic pain.
